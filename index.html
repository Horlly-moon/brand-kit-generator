Plate 04 — Process

Describe. Generate. Refine.

Three steps, one continuous loop. Every iteration is versioned and logged.

01

Describe

Name, industry, mood, colors to avoid — plus an optional logo, whose dominant colors are read in-browser and sent as seed tones. The image never leaves your machine.

02

Generate

The brief meets the live font catalog and real design constraints — 60-30-10 balance, color psychology, contrasting-but-readable pairing — and returns a full kit in about two minutes.

03

Refine

Nudge it in plain words — “more playful”, “swap the accent”. Only the requested change moves; everything else holds. Each version is logged to your history.

Plate 06 — Why it's different

Verifiable, not vibes.

Three claims you can check yourself on any kit it produces.

Fonts are sourced, not remembered

Both faces come from Google Fonts' live catalog fetched at generation time — the model is forbidden from inventing names. No more Oswald-everything.

Accessibility is computed

Text/background contrast is calculated per WCAG and auto-fixed in HSL when short of 4.5:1. A bad AI palette still can't ship an unreadable kit.

Every choice is argued

Each kit ships a written rationale tying palette and type back to your industry and mood — plus full version history when you refine.
