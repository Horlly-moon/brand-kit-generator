Process

Describe. Generate. Refine.

Three steps, one continuous loop. Every iteration is versioned and logged to your history.

01

Describe

Name, industry, mood, and colors to avoid, plus an optional logo. Logo colors are read in your browser and sent as seed tones. The image never leaves your machine.

02

Generate

Your brief meets the live font catalog and real design constraints: 60-30-10 balance, color psychology, and a contrasting but readable pairing. A full kit returns in about two minutes.

03

Refine

Nudge it in plain words. Try more playful or swap the accent. Only the requested change moves and everything else holds. Each version is logged to your history.

Why it is different

Verifiable, not vibes.

Three claims you can check yourself on any kit it produces.

Fonts are sourced, not remembered

Both faces come from Google Fonts live catalog fetched at generation time. The model is forbidden from inventing names, so you never get the same safe pairing twice.

Accessibility is computed

Text and background contrast is calculated per WCAG with a 4.5:1 floor, auto-fixed in HSL when short. A bad AI palette still cannot ship an unreadable kit.

Every choice is argued

Each kit ships a written rationale tying palette and type back to your industry and mood, plus full version history when you refine.
