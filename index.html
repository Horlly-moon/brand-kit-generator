Process

Describe. Generate. Refine.

Three steps, one continuous loop. Every iteration is versioned and logged to your history.

01

Describe

Name, industry, mood, and colors to avoid, plus an optional logo. Logo colors are read in your browser and sent as seed tones. The image never leaves your machine.

02

Generate

Your brief meets the live font catalog and real design constraints: 60-30-10 balance, color psychology, and a contrasting but readable pairing. A full kit returns in about two minutes.

03

Refine

Nudge it in plain words. Try more playful or swap the accent. Only the requested change moves and everything else holds. Each version is logged to your history.

05 / Begin

05Set your first kit.

Free while in the specimen stage. Takes about two minutes, and every version stays in your history.
